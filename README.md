## Start here

1. Copy a workbook into your own repository or download its Markdown file.
2. Fill in the problem and smallest useful flow. Keep untested ideas labeled as assumptions.
3. Try that flow with a potential user. Record what happened and what you changed.
4. Add a demo, instructions and evidence so someone else can check your work.

Prefer a guided start? Create a short brief in your browser. Choose an editable example for a first portfolio, recurring work, or a small business idea. Existing answers are kept; examples fill only empty fields:

## What this workbook is for

Students, early-career builders and people making their first small product with AI assistance. A spreadsheet, clickable sketch or one working page is a valid starting point. The objective is to make one useful flow, observe it in use and explain your decisions.

This repository contains learning templates, not completed learner portfolios. Filling them in is not certification or a promise of employment or revenue.

## Reuse

Copy and adapt the workbook for your own learning, portfolio or classroom. Keep a link to this repository when redistributing the original template. You own the content you add. Do not include confidential participant details in public examples.
